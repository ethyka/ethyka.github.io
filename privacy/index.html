Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

At AI Ethyka LLC, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile applications and software services.

2. Data Collection and Use

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the information necessary to provide and improve our services. Wherever possible, our artificial intelligence models process data locally on your device to ensure your information remains private and secure.

  • Diagnostic Data: We may collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability.
  • Personal Data: We do not collect or store personally identifiable information unless you explicitly provide it to us (e.g., contacting support).

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. We only partner with reputable services that adhere to strict data protection standards.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@aiethyka.com.
